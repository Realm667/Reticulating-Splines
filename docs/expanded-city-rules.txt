Expanded city rules — initial balance (2026-11-08)

Classic keeps the existing simulation. Founding offers Classic or Expanded.
An existing Classic city can switch to Expanded once, through Options >
Classic / Expanded. Conversion creates an independent SC2X working copy and
opens Save As. A running multiplayer session must be stopped before conversion.
There is no Expanded-to-Classic conversion.

New multi-city regions require Expanded, both at founding and when converting
an existing single city into a region. The New City dialog disables Region in
Classic; a new multiplayer region campaign selects Expanded for all cities.
Previously saved regions remain loadable with their stored rules unchanged.
The ten-year city chronicle is also Expanded-only (single-player, including
regions). Classic neither records years nor shows chronicle notices or its
menu entry. Existing archive data stays intact and becomes available after an
explicit switch to Expanded. Ordinary newspapers remain available in Classic.

Population includes arcology residents, counted within this city. Unlocks are
permanent even if population later falls. Each city may have one of each reward.
The palette shows unlocked buildings and the next future milestone. Original
rewards remain separate. Rebuilding uses the same construction price.

Placement requires flat, clear, unzoned land inside the map boundary. All cells
belong to one building. Demolition, undo, rotation and disaster cleanup preserve
that identity. Other structural tools cannot overwrite a reward parcel.

At each new calendar month, charge one twelfth of yearly upkeep. Newly placed
buildings start paying at the next month boundary. A failed payment disables
benefits until a later successful monthly payment. No debt or demolition is
triggered by unpaid upkeep. Power is also required for benefits. The building
query shows the operating state and upkeep; Budget shows a separate reward row.
All eight together cost 4,320 per year, or 360 per full month.

Effects use existing SC2K service and land-value equations:
- Museum: +25 college capacity, plus local land value up to +8 within 10 tiles.
- Opera: local land value up to +10 within 12 tiles.
- Park: local land value up to +16 within 20 tiles.
- Convention center: commercial land value up to +16 within 16 tiles.
- Hospital: +100 health capacity, equivalent to four fully funded hospitals.
- Library: +45 school capacity, equivalent to three fully funded schools.
- Research center: +100 college capacity, equivalent to two fully funded colleges.
- Exhibition: local land value up to +20 within 20 tiles.

Land-value strength falls linearly with Chebyshev distance from the footprint.
The strongest reward bonus applies; overlapping bonuses do not add or accumulate
on prior map values. Industry receives no reward land-value bonus. Other local
bonuses can benefit homes and commerce. These are bounded bonuses, not growth
multipliers or guaranteed development. Service funding uses the reward upkeep;
no additional edits to the original school/hospital budget controls are needed.

Save contract
SC2X required_features includes rs.expanded.v1. XEXP.bin contains EXP1 followed
by big-endian u32 fields: population peak (4), settled absolute month (8), unlock
mask (12), placement sequence counter (16), last monthly charge (20), year-to-date
charge (24), accounting year (28). Eight 16-byte records start at byte32: sequence
(0 means absent), x, y, funded flag. Total size160 bytes. Record order matches the
table above. XBLD uses inert statue carriers with no corner flags or XMIC reward
records; XEXP owns the actual identity. Older clients must reject the required
feature. Classic saves have no XEXP block. Expanded state is included in host
commands, snapshots, undo and region child documents; thresholds stay local.

Graphics
Eight GFX deliveries are shared by RS and SC2K; the latest opera geometry
correction is included. Day images, brightmaps and palette-index data ship as
built-in assets. Sprite bank4500000 holds the three views without using original
building byte IDs. Both the GPU and CPU map paths use the native painter.
SCURK MIF export retains only classic-compatible tiles. Asset hashes and authoring
provenance are in game/assets/expanded/provenance.json. Facility upgrades are
described below and preserve the existing building graphics.

BUILDING UPGRADES (Expanded only; initial balance, 2026-11-09)

Use Query > Upgrade building on an existing eligible facility. Each building
keeps its footprint, artwork, brightmaps, name and original microsimulation.
Gold stars show completed stages; a gray star shows construction. The marker
works with both graphics sets, rotation, zoom and night lighting.

Stage 1: local population peak 25,000 including arcologies, year 1920;
175% of the base construction price (rounded up), 75 game days (3 months).
Stage 2: peak 100,000, year 1950; another 250% of the base price, 150 days.
The city pays the full construction price when work starts. Completed output
remains in service during work. Work completes on the normal simulation clock.
There is no free upgrade, cancellation refund or third stage. Immediate Undo
uses the normal unchanged-city checks. Population decline does not relock a
stage already unlocked. Original invention dates and plant aging still apply.

Eligible facilities and effects:
- All power plants except wind: 150% / 200% of the existing generation formula.
  Hydroelectric plants are individual one-tile facilities and are eligible.
  Variable solar output uses the same random draws. Plant age is never reset;
  expiry and automatic replacement remove the upgrade. Query shows remaining life.
- Water towers: 150% / 200% storage; desalination: 150% / 200% production;
  water treatment: 150% / 200% treatment capacity. Water pumps are excluded.
  Expanded tank cells store 150 / 200 units. A component with upgraded tanks
  fills only complete cells. Commissioning empties the enlarged tank, which
  refills from actual surplus; enlarging stored-water bits cannot create water.
  Components without upgraded tanks retain the original storage rounding.
- Police/fire stations: 150% / 200% coverage strength using the existing kernel,
  footprint and funding/power penalties. No doubled radius. Coverage still caps
  at the original data-map maximum.
- Hospitals, schools, colleges: 150% / 200% service capacity, with the original
  funding controls. Integer capacity is rounded down at the existing boundary.
- Prisons: 150% / 200% prisoner capacity and corresponding crowding thresholds.
- Libraries: +5 / +10 school capacity; museums: +5 / +10 college capacity,
  each controlled by the corresponding existing school/college funding slider.
  These additions deliberately affect education, not just annual query counters.
- Stadiums and zoos: 150% / 200% of their existing residential growth allowance.
- Bus depots, rail stations and subway stations: station traversal/transfer
  cost falls from 4 to 3 to 2 in actual trip finding and reach analysis.
  This is a transport improvement, not a fictitious passenger capacity meter.

There are no upgrades for wind, pumps, grown zones, network tiles, automatic
port/airport pieces, legacy rewards or the eight metropolis rewards.

The original building upkeep remains in the existing budget categories.
Additional upkeep is 50% / 100% of the original annual amount, at current
funding. No operating charge is invented for a building with no original charge.
Fully funded annual base amounts: police/fire 100, hospital 50, school 25,
college 100, bus depot 25, rail station 1.60, subway station 0.40 dollars.
The extra is charged monthly with per-building fractional carry. A completed
stage starts its new rate the next month. The budget has a separate Building
upgrades row for the extra cost; its forecast includes fractional carry and
scheduled construction completions. If the extra charge cannot be paid, only the
extra benefit stops until the next affordable monthly charge; the base service
continues and no debt or arrears are silently created. Funding still controls
the underlying service. Demolition/destruction loses work and stars without a
refund. A newly constructed building on the same parcel starts at stage zero.

Coop commands are checked and applied by the host. Shared additionally checks
the complete parcel and pays from the acting municipality. A complete land
sale transfers stage, construction and fractional carry to the new owner with
new local identities. Foreign stars are display metadata and never enter the
viewer's upkeep. Region accepts upgrades only in the player's own active city.
Repeated and stale commands cannot charge twice. Original Classic cities have
no upgrade data or effects. Existing Expanded cities gain the capability when
opened; saving then requires a client that supports rs.upgrades.v1.

XUPG persistence (all integer fields unsigned big-endian u32):
Required feature: rs.upgrades.v1, in addition to rs.expanded.v1.
Header: 24 bytes: UPG1, last identity, settled month, last monthly extra charge,
year-to-date extra charge, accounting year (age in days / 300).
Each 40-byte record: identity, origin X, origin Y, original tile ID, completed
stage, target stage (0 = idle), completion day (0 = idle), paid flag, fractional
charge carry (1/12000 dollar), original facility overlay ID (0 without one).
Records must have unique identities and valid, non-overlapping complete
footprints matching the original building and facility. Unsupported capability,
missing chunks, malformed data and stale identities are rejected. The original
map occupancy and microsimulation remain authoritative. Rotation updates origin
and directional hydro tile ID. Undo restores the complete relevant chunks.
